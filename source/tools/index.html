Small tools I've built for my own use. The browser tools need no install -- just open and go, with all progress in localStorage. The desktop tools are open-source and available on GitHub.

🎯 Quizzer

Flashcards that know what you're about to forget. Load up a vocab deck, study in your browser, and let the app figure out what to quiz you on next. Tracks your progress over time.

🌍 Translator

Practice translating sentences and get instant AI feedback -- what you got right, what sounds off, and how to say it better. Supports 8 languages and gets harder as you improve.

📅 SetDate

Mac utility for batch-setting or shifting timestamps on photo and video files. Updates EXIF metadata and filesystem creation date so your photo library sorts things correctly.

📝 MDE (Markdown Editor)

A simple but sturdy visual markdown text editor, inspired by Obsidian and VS Code. Standard editing tools, tabs, file explorer, outline view, quick-open, and DOCX/PDF import/export.
